HOW TO PLAY:

  • A verse will be displayed with a geographical location in it.
  • This location will be concealed.
  • Using your knowledge of the Bible and context from the surrounding verses, find the location from the verse on the map!
  • Closer guesses score more points.
  • The daily game has 3 rounds, after which you can share your score with friends!
